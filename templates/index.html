Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

THREAD.AI collects and processes your chat interactions, uploaded datasets, and basic profile information (name and email provided via Google Authentication) to provide personalized textile intelligence and improve our AI responses.

2. Data Usage

Your data is exclusively used to process your direct queries. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Uploaded datasets (e.g., CSV files) are temporarily processed to generate insights and are not permanently stored.

3. Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your information. All API communications with our underlying language models are encrypted securely in transit.

4. User Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your data. You can clear your chat history at any time using the "Delete" function, and wipe your session completely by signing out of your account.
